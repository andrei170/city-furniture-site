Our story

Built by one family, brick by brick.

City Furniture started with a single store in Prince George and a simple promise: treat every customer like a guest in your home. It began with hard work, little more than a dream, and the trust of the community.

From nothing, into the stores three generations of families have grown up shopping in.

Fifty years on, the same family still runs it, store by store, across Western Canada. Every location is locally owned by people who live where you live.
